# Sunburst brand

The visual direction is minimal, bold, playful, polished and positive.

- Paper: `#FFF8EE`
- Ink: `#111111`
- Orange: `#FF7A1A`
- Red: `#EF3E24`
- Highlight yellow: `#FFD84D`
- Display type: Fraunces
- Body and captions: Archivo

Use orange-to-red gradients as emphasis, not wallpaper. Pair one expressive serif statement with clean sans-serif support. Keep generous negative space, hard visual hierarchy, rounded editorial cards and confident black rules.

Motion should feel smooth and cinematic: fast reveal, gentle settle, readable hold, clean exit. Use full-frame paper cards for major idea changes, layered logos or text around the subject for product mentions, and oversized foreground words sparingly.

Captions may pair serif emphasis with sans-serif context. Keep them clear of faces and platform controls. Design quality matters more than graphic density; every element must clarify or strengthen a beat.
